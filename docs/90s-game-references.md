# 90'S GAMING

------------------------------
[1]
[[Z](https://www.google.com/search?q=z&kgmid=/m/0845g_) (1996): Real-time military strategy reimagined as a beer-chugging, cigar-smoking robot cartoon.](<https://www.google.com/search?q=z&kgmid=/m/0845g>_)

[2]
[[Cannon Fodder](https://www.google.com/search?q=cannon+fodder&kgmid=/m/0k94zj7) (1993): Adorable, addictive war gameplay paired with an intentionally jarring, pitch-black critique of wartime mortality.](<https://www.google.com/search?q=cannon+fodder&kgmid=/m/0k94zj7>)

------------------------------
[3]
[[MDK](https://www.google.com/search?q=mdk&kgmid=/m/03w9q7) (1997): A janitor in a face-sniper suit fighting city-sized alien mining ships with weaponized absurdity.](<https://www.google.com/search?q=mdk&kgmid=/m/03w9q7>)

------------------------------
[4]
[[Earthworm Jim](https://www.google.com/search?q=earthworm+jim&kgmid=/m/0swcs) (1994): Surreal, high-budget Saturday morning cartoon logic converted directly into a video game.](<https://www.google.com/search?q=earthworm+jim&kgmid=/m/0swcs>)

[5]
[[Mutant League Hockey/Football](https://www.google.com/search?q=mutant+league+hockey/football&kgmid=/m/04n3bk) (1993/1994): Taking EA's serious sports engines and introducing literal landmines, bribery, and murdering the referee.](<https://www.google.com/search?q=mutant+league+hockey/football&kgmid=/m/04n3bk>)

[6]
[[Carmageddon](https://www.google.com/search?q=carmageddon&kgmid=/m/01783h) (1997): Turning a racing game into an open-world sandbox of vehicular destruction and pitch-black shock humor.](<https://www.google.com/search?q=carmageddon&kgmid=/m/01783h>)

[7]
[[Postal](https://www.google.com/search?q=postal&kgmid=/m/04bf6m) (1997): The raw, anti-establishment, grunge-era counter-culture interactive breakdown.](<https://www.google.com/search?q=postal&kgmid=/m/04bf6m>)

[8]
[[NBA Jam](https://www.google.com/search?q=nba+jam&kgmid=/m/01f8k9) (1993): Defying the laws of physics and sports simulation for pure, unadulterated arcade hype.](<https://www.google.com/search?q=nba+jam&kgmid=/m/01f8k9>)
